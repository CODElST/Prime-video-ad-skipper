# Privacy Policy: Ad Control for Prime Video

_Last updated: 23 November 2026_

Ad Control for Prime Video does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

**What the extension does with data**
- It reads the Prime Video page you have open to detect when an ad is playing, and controls the page's video (mute, playback speed, seeking). This happens entirely inside your browser.
- It saves two on/off settings ("Jump to end of ad" and "Speed up at 16x") using the browser's local extension storage. These settings stay on your device.

**What it does not do**
- It makes no network requests and sends no data to the developer or to any third party.
- It uses no analytics, tracking, advertising or remote code.
- It does not read or store your viewing history, account details or page content.
